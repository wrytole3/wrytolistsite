Help & Support

Last updated: 13 October 2026

Accounts

How do I create an account?

Open the account page and choose the create-account option. Use an email address you can access and keep your password private.

Why do I need to sign in to download?

Downloads are intended to be linked to a user account so the platform can apply access rules and help users manage their activity.

Documents

Why can’t I open or download a document?

The file may be unavailable, still awaiting approval, or require sign-in or paid access. Check the message shown on the page and confirm you are using the correct account.

Can I view free documents without subscribing?

The intended rule is that free documents can be viewed without a site subscription. A download requires an account. Paid documents may show a limited preview before full access is granted.

Uploads

Why is my upload not appearing publicly?

Check the upload status in your dashboard. Some resources may need approval, and an upload is not complete if the file transfer or record creation failed.

Payments

Does submitting a payment reference activate access automatically?

No. A submitted reference is a request for review, not proof that the payment has been verified. Keep your transaction confirmation and wait for confirmation of access.

Still need help?

Contact support with a short description of the issue. Do not share your password, payment PIN or secret authentication codes.
