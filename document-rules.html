Document & Payment Rules

Last updated: 13 October 2026

Access model: The rules below describe the intended policy. They apply technically only when the relevant viewer, backend checks and payment verification are implemented and enabled on the live site.

1. Free documents

  • Free documents are intended to be viewable in full without a Wrytole site subscription.
  • Users must sign in or create an account before downloading a document.
  • Free status does not remove the requirement to respect copyright and the permitted use of the material.

2. Paid documents

  • Visitors may see a preview of the first two pages.
  • Opening page three or accessing the complete document requires the access or payment specified on that document.
  • Users must sign in before downloading, and the platform must confirm the required access before delivering a paid file.

3. Payment verification

  • Follow the payment instructions shown on the site.
  • Keep the transaction confirmation and submit the correct reference if requested.
  • Submitting a reference is not the same as verified payment. Access should be activated only after verification succeeds.
  • Do not send passwords, one-time authentication codes or mobile-money PINs to anyone.

4. Accounts and sharing

Do not share account credentials, attempt to bypass paid access or distribute protected files without permission. Access may be limited or removed where misuse is detected.

5. Educator uploads

Teachers and schools must have the rights needed to share uploaded material and must provide accurate titles, subjects, class details and pricing. Resources may be reviewed before publication and may be removed if they violate platform rules or rights.

6. Errors or disputes

If a payment is made but access is not activated, contact support and provide the transaction reference and relevant account email. Never provide your payment PIN. If a document is incorrect or unavailable, include its title and a short description of the issue.
