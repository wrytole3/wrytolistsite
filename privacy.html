Privacy Policy

Last updated: 13 October 2026

Policy status: This is a plain-language policy draft for the platform. Before launch, the site owner should confirm the actual data flows, retention periods, contact route and any third-party services used.

Information that may be processed

Depending on the features you use, Wrytole may process account information such as your name, email address, account role and school details; resource information such as titles and uploaded files; and records needed to manage access, support requests and payment-reference reviews.

How information is used

  • To create and secure accounts and provide the requested features.
  • To store, organise, review and publish educational resources according to permissions.
  • To check document access, manage subscriptions and review payment references.
  • To respond to support requests, prevent misuse and maintain platform reliability.

Service providers

The current website uses Supabase for certain authentication, database and storage functions. Other providers may process information when their features are enabled. Their own terms and privacy notices may also apply.

Payments

Use only the payment process shown by the platform. Do not send your payment PIN or password to Wrytole. A payment reference may be stored to help review a transaction; submitting it does not itself verify payment.

Sharing and security

Wrytole should limit access to personal information to authorised purposes and use appropriate access controls. No online system can guarantee absolute security. Publicly approved resources may be visible to website visitors; private account and payment-review information should not be public.

Retention and your choices

Information should be retained only as needed for account operation, legitimate records, safety and legal obligations. To ask about correcting or deleting account information, contact Wrytole support. Some records may need to be retained where required to resolve disputes or comply with applicable obligations.

Children and school accounts

Schools, guardians and users should provide only information necessary for learning and account operation. School accounts should manage student information responsibly and only within their authorised permissions.

Policy changes

This policy may be updated as the platform changes. The updated date at the top indicates the latest revision of this page.

Contact Wrytole about privacy questions.
